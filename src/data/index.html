Key bindings

Combine modifiers with +, for example CTRL+SHIFT+P. Single characters, F1-F24, ESC TAB ENTER SPACE BACKSPACE DELETE HOME END PGUP PGDN UP DOWN LEFT RIGHT and media keys VOL_UP VOL_DOWN MUTE PLAY STOP NEXT PREV are recognised. Leave a field empty to disable the key. The key you press on the pad is highlighted here.
